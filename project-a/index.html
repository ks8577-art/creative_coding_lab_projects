Krakel

Scientific Name:

Krakelus singularis (Krakel)

Discovered by / at:

Karanjot Singh, in the Violet Nebula, Fall 2026

Family:

Vortacephalidae. Krakel is the only one of its kind. Scientists think it is related to octopuses and squids that somehow ended up living in space.

Habitat:

Deep space, close to nebulas. It mostly stays in the upper part of the sky.

Appearance:

Krakel has a small purple head with one big eye. The eye is red with a green slit pupil, and it follows you wherever you go. It has a mouth full of sharp teeth with an overbite, and long strands hanging under its chin. It has six big tentacles, two tentacle hands with spears at the end, and small green glowing spots all over its body.

Key Characteristics:

  • Warps space like a black hole.
  • Pulls in stars and nebula clouds.
  • Its eye follows you.
  • Shoots red lasers.
  • Blinks every few seconds.
  • Weak to blue lasers.

What we know so far about Krakel? Pilots flying through the Violet Nebula used to say that the stars there moved on their own. Nobody believed them until Fall 2026, when Karanjot Singh found out what was causing it. It was Krakel.

Krakel has its own gravity, kind of like a small black hole. When it floats by, the stars and the nebula clouds around it get pulled in and twisted. Once it moves away, everything goes back to normal.

Krakel does not chase you. It just floats at the top of the sky and watches. If it sees you moving, it shoots red lasers at you. The only thing that can hurt it is blue light.

Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Move your mouse to dodge Krakel's red lasers. Click to shoot a blue laser straight up. You have 3 hearts and Krakel has 10 health, shown in the top-left corner. You get a few seconds at the start before it begins shooting. When the game ends, click to play again.
